# ADR 0003: Contextual tracks and separation product boundary

Status: accepted.

Dozi will continue to treat the generated master as the primary production asset. The application will not present ACE-Step Lego, ACE-Step Extract, or the evaluated dedicated separator as a dependable multitrack reconstruction system, and it will not build a mixer foundation around their current output quality.

ACE-Step Lego may be exposed later as an explicitly experimental creative-regeneration workflow. Its outputs remain `NATIVE_TRACK` assets with `GENERATED_NATIVE` provenance and `LEGO_CONTEXTUAL` generation method because they are newly generated alternatives, not isolated performances. Multiple candidates, automatic rejection of obvious failures, and final human selection are required by the observed seed dependence.

Extract and dedicated source separation produce `DERIVED_STEM` assets linked to their source. They may support provisional editing or in-context remix workflows, but the product must not promise pristine solo tracks. A separator must pass representative clean-reference benchmarks, blinded listening, and model-weight licensing review before production adoption.

Exact duration is not evidence of musical synchronization. Product promotion requires section-level timing, tempo, leakage, instrument identity, and audible-artifact acceptance criteria. Provider capabilities and UI labels must distinguish creative regeneration from faithful separation.

This decision preserves the existing multi-asset schema and provider boundary so future validated implementations do not require a data-model rewrite. It postpones mixer UI, fine-grained drum decomposition, and production stem claims until evidence supports them.
